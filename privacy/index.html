Privacy Policy - Sonar Katta

Last updated: 2 October 2026

Sonar Katta (@sonarkatta) publishes daily gold and silver rates in Marathi. This page explains how our Instagram rates auto-reply works and what information it handles.

What the service does

When you comment a rates keyword (for example "RATE") on one of our Instagram posts or reels, or message our Instagram account, our automation can send you a private message with the latest gold and silver rates. It may ask you to follow @sonarkatta first. It replies only after you comment or message us. We do not send unrequested broadcasts.

Information we handle

  • Your public Instagram comment text and your Instagram-scoped user ID, to decide whether to reply.
  • The text of your message to us, to decide what to send.
  • Whether your account follows @sonarkatta, only to decide whether to send the rates.

We do not collect your name, phone number, email address, location, or payment details through this service.

Storage and sharing

We keep only technical identifiers (comment and message IDs) for up to 14 days so that we do not reply twice. We do not store message conversations for any other purpose. We do not sell, rent, or share your information with third parties, except Meta Platforms (Instagram), which delivers the messages, and our hosting provider, which runs the automation.

Data deletion

To ask us to delete anything related to you, send a message to @sonarkatta on Instagram with the words "delete my data". Technical identifiers expire automatically within 14 days.

Contact

Instagram: @sonarkatta
